# Horizon Creature (library)

The library every mod of the [Horizon Creature](../README.md) series needs. It adds no creature of its own, but what
the creatures share:

- **The creature guide**, given to every player the first time they join a world. Right-click it to open the
  **bestiary**: each creature you come across gets a page, turning in 3D, with the biomes you met it in, how rare it is
  and what you found out about it. A lost one is crafted again from a book, a feather and leather.
- **The glass jar** (3 glass in a U and an oak slab, makes 2), with Butterflies, Fireflies or Worms installed: it
  catches their creatures and releases them where you want them to stay. Sneak + right-click places a filled jar on
  display.
- **The "Horizon Creature" creative tab**, with every item of the series.
- Monsters leave the series' creatures alone.
