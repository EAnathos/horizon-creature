# Horizon Creature — library specification (v0.2)

The mandatory library of the Horizon Creature series ([docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)): the systems
several creature mods share, and the series' single creative tab. It adds no creature of its own.

> ✅ = decided · ❓ = proposal to confirm · numbers are starting values, to balance in game.

## 1. Role and rules ✅

- Every mod of the series **requires** the library; the library depends on no mod.
- A system belongs here when **two or more mods use it or are specified to use it** (the rule of two), or when it ties
  the series together (the tab). Otherwise it stays in its mod until a second mod needs it.
- The library holds **mechanisms**, the mods hold **species**: no entity type in `creature:`. A mod gives the library its
  species and numbers (registration at start, or parameters).
- A library feature that only makes sense with some mods is **switched on by them** (registration, conditional recipe),
  e.g. the glass jar (§6).
- A system moved here keeps working for the mod it came from, in the same commit.

## 2. Technical context

- **Mod id / namespace**: `creature`. Name: "Horizon Creature".
- **Native half**: a trait mod with an `[api]` crate (`mod-creature`), which the mods reach as the `creature` crate.
  Its `hooks()` runs before theirs (dependency order), so a mod registers its species from its own `hooks()`. It
  publishes functions, types and statics; shared state is behind `Mutex`es.
- **Content half**: the series' tab, shared blocks and items (`creature:incubator`, `creature:glass_jar`), their recipes
  (conditional when a feature depends on a mod), textures and translations (`en_us`, `fr_fr`).
- **Pinned** to the series' Horizon revision (docs/CONVENTIONS.md). Version `0.1.0`; a breaking API change bumps the
  minor version while in `0.x`.

## 3. Systems in place

### 3.1 Model drawing (`creature::model`) ✅

- `Part` / `Cube`: a Blockbench model as data. `cargo creature model <mod> [model]` converts
  `mods/<mod>/assets/models/<model>.bbmodel` into a `pub static <MODEL>: Part` in the mod's crate.
- `draw(host, draw, part, texture, pose)`: draws the tree with the mod's texture; `pose(group)` gives each group's
  animation for the frame. Called from the mod's `mob_submit` hook, which then cancels the template's model.
- Users: vulture; every future mob of the series.

### 3.2 Mob sounds (`creature::sounds`) ✅

- Our mobs copy a vanilla mob whose sounds they must not play, and Horizon prefixes mob sounds with `minecraft:`. The mod
  mutes its mob and the library plays the mod's own sounds with `Host::play_sound`.
- `queue(mob, sound, volume, pitch)` from goals; `tick_post(host, event, voice, source)` from the mod's
  `entity_tick_post`: plays the queue, and the `Voice`'s hurt and death sounds by watching the mob's health.
- Users: vulture; every future mob.

### 3.3 Creative tab ✅

- One tab, "Horizon Creature" (`itemGroup.creature`), generated from the modules' `module.toml`
  (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §4). The library's items first, then each mod's.
- Icon: `minecraft:sniffer_egg` until the incubator exists, then `creature:incubator` ❓.
- The glass jar is listed even when no mod uses it (a tab can't be conditional); it is then inert and uncraftable.

## 4. Incubator ✅

A block that hatches the eggs of any mod of the series. Users: **Wyverns**, **Snakes**; any future mod with eggs.
Eggs hatch **only in the incubator** ✅ (a placed egg does nothing).

### 4.1 Gameplay

- **Block** `creature:incubator`, a furnace-like block with a screen. Recipe ❓ (proposal: 4 iron ingots around, 2 hay
  bales, 1 furnace in the middle, 2 glass panes on top).
- **One egg** at a time ✅: one egg slot, one fuel slot.
- **Fuel** ✅: anything that burns in a furnace (coal, charcoal, wood, lava bucket, blaze rod…), with the furnace's burn
  times.
- **Temperature** ✅: the player **sets** the incubator's temperature on its screen (− / + buttons) on a scale of
  **5 levels** ❓:

  | Level ❓ | Name | Fuel use (× furnace) ❓ |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Tepid | ×0.5 |
  | 2 | Warm | ×1 |
  | 3 | Hot | ×1.5 |
  | 4 | Very hot | ×2.5 |
  | 5 | Scorching | ×4 |

  **The hotter, the more fuel it burns** ✅. The actual temperature climbs or falls one level every 10 s ❓ towards the
  setting while there is fuel, and falls when the fuel runs out.
- Each egg needs **one specific temperature** ✅. If the incubator's actual temperature is not the egg's while the egg is
  in it, the egg **cracks** ✅ (it is lost: shell particles and a crack sound; nothing returned ❓). A short grace ❓ (5 s)
  covers the moment the egg is put in while the temperature is still climbing — or the egg only starts and only checks
  once the temperature first matches ❓.
- The screen shows the setting, the actual temperature, the fuel left and the progress. The egg's tooltip names its
  temperature ("Needs: Warm") ❓, so the player knows what to set.
- **Owner** ✅: the player who put the egg in. The creature hatches next to the incubator, as a baby, tamed to its owner.
- Hoppers: fuel from the side, like a furnace ❓; eggs only by hand ❓.

### 4.2 Registration (native API)

Each mod registers its eggs at start, from its `hooks()`:

```rust
creature::incubator::register(EggKind {
    item: "snakes:snake_egg",
    hatches: "snakes:snake",
    temperature: 2,          // Warm
    ticks: 12_000,           // 10 min
    named: true,             // §5: the owner names it before it hatches
    on_hatch: Some(snake_hatched), // the mod sets its colours, pattern, owner data…
});
```

- The library knows nothing of the species: egg item, entity, temperature, time and naming come from the mod. An egg it
  doesn't know is refused by the slot.
- Eggs are **items in the mod's namespace**; the incubator, its screen and its rules are the library's.

### 4.3 Egg data

- An egg item may carry data (an item component ❓) set when it drops: the parent's colours and pattern (§7) or stats, so
  the baby inherits them ❓. Otherwise the baby rolls its own.

### 4.4 Technical notes

- Horizon allows a mod block with a block entity, a container menu (`native_block_entity_container`), a mod screen with
  buttons (`native_screen`, `native_menu_button`) and a machine tick (`native_machines.rs`): to prototype first.
  Spawning the baby: `native_summon` with its data.

## 5. Naming on hatch ✅

- An egg registered with `named: true` (Snakes ✅) does not hatch when its time is up: it **waits, ready** (it no longer
  needs the temperature ❓), until its owner **gives it a name** on the incubator's screen; then it hatches, bearing that
  name ✅.
- Proposal ❓: a text field and a "Hatch" button on the incubator screen. If Horizon's mod screens have no text field
  yet, the fallback is a **name tag** renamed on an anvil and put into the egg slot's side slot ❓.
- Reusable by any mod (Wyverns ❓).

## 6. Glass jar and territories ✅

Catching small creatures and settling them in a place they won't leave. Users: **Butterflies**, **Worms** ✅. The jar is
the library's ✅ and is **active only if Butterflies or Worms is installed** ✅.

### 6.1 The jar

- Item `creature:glass_jar`. Recipe ✅ conditional (`neoforge:conditions`, `mod_loaded` butterflies **or** worms):
  proposal ❓ 3 glass in a U + 1 oak slab lid (gives 2 ❓).
- **Catch** ✅: right-click a catchable creature with an empty jar → a **filled jar** holding it (its colours, pattern and
  name are kept).
- The filled jar is an item of the creature's mod (`butterflies:butterfly_jar`, `worms:worm_jar`) ❓, so each mod has its
  own texture and name, while catching and releasing are the library's. A mod registers its catchable kinds:

  ```rust
  creature::jar::register(Catchable { kind: "worms:worm", jar: "worms:worm_jar", territory: Some(GARDEN) });
  ```

- **Release** ✅: right-click a block with a filled jar → the creature comes out there and the jar is empty again. If the
  creature has a territory rule (§6.2) it **settles** there.
- **Display** ❓: sneak + right-click places the filled jar as a small block (a creature visibly inside, glowing faintly
  for butterflies ❓), broken back into the item. Decorative.

### 6.2 Territories ✅

A settled creature **stays in an area** around one or several blocks, and never despawns ✅. The area is computed from
the block it was released on, by the mod's rule:

| Rule | User | Area |
|---|---|---|
| **Garden** ✅ | Worms | The farmland connected to the release block (4 directions, same level ±1); **at least 10 blocks** ✅, otherwise the worm won't settle (it digs away ❓) |
| **Flower patch** ❓ | Butterflies | The flowers connected to the release flower (each within 3 blocks of another), plus 3 blocks around them and 4 blocks up |

- The creature remembers its anchor (saved on the entity); every few seconds ❓ it checks the area still holds (the
  garden is still ≥ 10 farmland, the flowers are still there). If not, it becomes unsettled (wanders, may despawn ❓).
- A mod may add a rule (a jar on a shelf, a pond… ❓).

## 7. Colours and patterns ✅

One species with **several colours and patterns** ✅. Users: **Butterflies**, **Snakes**.

- A creature rolls a **base colour**, a **pattern** and a **pattern colour** at spawn (from lists the mod gives, each
  weighted, per biome ❓). Saved on the entity, kept in jars and eggs.
- Drawn as two layers: a base texture tinted by the base colour, then the pattern texture tinted by the pattern colour
  (`draw_cube` takes a colour), like vanilla's tropical fish. So 8 colours × 6 patterns × 8 colours need only 7
  textures.
- Offspring (eggs) take one parent's colours and pattern, with a small mutation chance ❓.
