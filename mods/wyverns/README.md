# Wyverns

A mod of the [Horizon Creature](../../README.md) series: a dark and mystical dimension of harpies and wyverns, ruled by the Harpy Queen; wyvern eggs hatch in the library's incubator and the young wyverns can be raised and ridden.

**Requires** Horizon Creature (`creature`), the series' library. With Butterflies or Cyclops installed, the staff that
opens the dimension is crafted with butterfly dust (else gunpowder) or a cyclops eye (else an eye of ender).

> **Status:** planned. Only the specification exists: [SPECS.md](SPECS.md). Nothing is implemented yet.

When implementation starts, this folder gets `content/` and `native/` like [the vulture's](../vulture/README.md)
(see [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §7](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#7-adding-a-mod)).
