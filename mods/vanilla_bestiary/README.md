# Vanilla Bestiary

An add-on of the [Horizon Creature](../../README.md) series, **planned**: Minecraft's own creatures get pages in the
creature guide's bestiary, found out in play like the series' creatures (where you met them, what they eat, what they
give). **Requires** Horizon Creature, the series' library.

Not playable yet: the design is in [SPECS.md](SPECS.md).
