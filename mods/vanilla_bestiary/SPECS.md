# Vanilla Bestiary: specification

An add-on of the **Sunrise Creatures** series for **Minecraft Horizon** (Minecraft Java 26.3): Minecraft's own creatures
get pages in the creature guide's bestiary, found out in play like the series' creatures. It adds no creature and no
item ✅.

**Requires** the series' library, Sunrise Creatures (`creature`, [common/SPECS.md](../../common/SPECS.md)), whose book
it fills (§7). Like every module of the series, it needs no other mod.

> ✅ = decided · ❓ = proposal to confirm · numbers are starting values, to balance in game.

## 0. Status

In development: written, and tested in game in single player (meeting wild ones, the spawn egg turning on the page,
breeding, milk, wool, drops, a spawn egg's creature tagged not wild); the other facts of §3 are still to see in game.

- **The creatures** (`native/api/src/creatures.rs`): 83 of the 88 with a spawn egg, each with its rarity, where it
  lives besides its natural spawns, and its facts. Left out: the copper and snow golems (built), the happy ghast
  (grown), the mule (only ever bred).
- **The game's data** (`native/api/src/game_data.rs`, generated by `cargo creature vanilla` from the player's 26.3 jar):
  each creature's natural spawns (each biome's `minecraft:gameplay/natural_mob_spawns`), what its loot table can drop,
  and the first item of its `<creature>_food` tag.
- **Wild ones only**: a creature hatched from a spawn egg, bred or tamed carries the tag `vanilla_bestiary.not_wild`
  (one tamed before the mod was installed is known by its owner). One made by `/summon` still counts as wild.
- **The facts** (§3), found out through Horizon's hooks.
- **The library's side** (common/SPECS.md §7, version 0.1.1): `bestiary::draw_icon`, biomes as `@` and a dimension,
  icon-only facts and facts wrapped onto several lines, Minecraft's creatures listed below a line, the right page
  scrolled with the wheel.

Next: the rest of §4 in game (taming, a bucket, an egg laid, a creeper, a transformation, the scrolling); then the
library's book tabs (common/SPECS.md §7.1).

## 1. Technical context

- **Mod id**: `vanilla_bestiary` ✅. A native mod only (`native/`), plus its translations (`content/`, the facts' text
  in English and French). No entity, no item.
- Uses from the library: the bestiary (§7): it registers one species per vanilla creature (`creature::bestiary::
  register`), with its biomes, its facts, its spawn egg as icon and its rarity.
- **No 3D view** ✅: the book's 3D view draws blocks and our own models (`Host::draw_scene`), not a vanilla mob's model,
  at our Horizon pin. A vanilla creature's page shows its spawn egg, large and turning, in place of it
  (`creature::bestiary::draw_icon` as its `Species::draw`, drawn with `Host::draw_item`), until Horizon can draw an
  entity in a scene (a request to file, docs/HORIZON-ISSUES.md).
- **The game's data** ✅: `cargo creature vanilla [jar]` reads the biomes, drops and breeding foods from the game's jar
  (default: the launcher's 26.3) into `game_data.rs`. Run it again when the series moves to another Minecraft version.

## 2. Which creatures ✅

- **Every vanilla creature that spawns on its own** ✅, the Overworld's, the Nether's and the End's: animals (cow, pig,
  sheep, chicken, horse, wolf, cat, fox, bee, axolotl, frog, camel, sniffer, armadillo...), water creatures, villagers
  and the wandering trader, monsters (zombie, creeper, skeleton, enderman, the illagers...), and the bosses (the Ender
  Dragon, the Wither, the Warden, the elder guardian). Not the player, armour stands or other objects.
- Each with its **biomes** (where it spawns on its own, from the game's data; for the creatures of structures, raids
  and bosses, the mod's list, or a whole dimension: an evoker anywhere in the Overworld, a blaze anywhere in the
  Nether), its **rarity** (common to very rare) and its **spawn egg** as icon (26.3 has one for every creature, the
  Ender Dragon's and the Wither's included).
- Not the creatures a player makes: the copper and snow golems, the happy ghast, the mule ✅.
- Met as the series' creatures are: a wild one within a few blocks, in one of its biomes (one hatched from a spawn egg,
  bred or tamed doesn't count as wild ✅; one made by `/summon` does, as Horizon doesn't say).

## 3. Facts ✅

What a player finds out about each, in play, each a word and an item icon on its page, as the series' facts:

- **Food / breeding**: fed the item that breeds it (wheat to a cow, seeds to a chicken, a golden carrot to a horse...).
- **Taming**: tamed (a wolf with a bone, a cat with fish, a horse ridden...).
- **Products**: what it gives without being killed (milk from a cow, wool from a sheep, a turtle's scute, honey from a
  bee's hive...).
- **Drops**: what it dropped when killed by the player, each item once.
- **Special**: one per creature where it has one (a creeper explodes, an enderman takes blocks, a piglin trades for
  gold, a villager's trades, a goat rams).

Found through Horizon's hooks; a fact whose event a mod can't see at our pin is left out until it can ✅:

| Fact | Hook | Who learns it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breeding | `entity_enter_love_mode` | the player who fed it |
| Taming | `entity_tame` | its new owner |
| Products: an item used on it (milk, stew) | `interact_entity` | that player |
| Products: shears or a brush (wool, mushrooms, a scute) | `loot_drops` with `used` | that player |
| Products: a bucket (fish, axolotl, tadpole) | `player_bucket_entity` | that player |
| Products: dropped on its own (an egg, a scute, a goat horn, a sniffer's seeds, a panda's sneeze) | `entity_drop_item` | the players near |
| Drops, each item once | `loot_drops` of a mob a player killed | its killer |
| Special: a creeper explodes | `explosion_prime` where a creeper stood | the players near |
| Special: an enderman takes a block | `entity_change_block` | the players near |
| Special: a piglin barters | `entity_drop_item` of a piglin | the players near |
| Special: it turns into another (a zombie drowns, a zombie villager is cured, a pig or a villager struck by lightning, a skeleton freezes, a tadpole grows, a hoglin zombifies, a husk drowns) | `entity_transform` | the players near |

The drops are on their own lines below the other facts, under the heading "Drops": each its item alone, with no
word.

## 4. Testing in game

`cargo creature install`, a new world in survival:

- Meet cows, sheep, zombies: their pages open below a line under the series' creatures, with their spawn egg turning
  and their biome. A cow hatched from a spawn egg doesn't open a page.
- Feed wheat to a cow (Breeding), milk it (Milk), shear a sheep (Wool), catch a cod in a bucket (Bucket), kill a
  zombie (its drops as items), see a creeper explode and a chicken lay an egg.
- More than 25 species met: the wheel scrolls the right page.
